Tracklog

Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Tracklog has no server and no accounts. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads.

Your library file

When you upload Library.xml, your browser reads it and builds a database in this tab's memory. The file and the database are never uploaded anywhere. Closing or reloading the tab deletes them.

Cover art lookups

To show album covers, Tracklog sends the title, artist and album of the songs on screen to Apple's public iTunes Search service, and loads the cover images from Apple. Apple sees those searches and your IP address, as with any website request. Their handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Turn off Show cover art in the sidebar to stop these requests.

Stored in your browser

  • Cover image links that were already found, so they don't have to be searched again.
  • Whether you turned cover art off.

These stay on your device. Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove them.

Other services the page loads

  • The Inter font from Google Fonts (Google's privacy policy).
  • The SQLite engine (sql.js) from the jsDelivr CDN.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits for security (GitHub's privacy statement).
