Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Tracklog has no server and no accounts. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads.

Your library file

When you upload Library.xml, your browser reads it and builds a database in this tab's memory. The file and the database are never uploaded anywhere. Closing or reloading the tab deletes them.

You can check this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and upload a file. No request carries it.

Cover art lookups

Your library file has no pictures in it. To show album covers for your own library, Tracklog can send the title, artist and album of the songs on screen to Apple's public iTunes Search service, and load the cover images from Apple. It asks you first, and sends nothing until you choose Find covers. Apple sees those searches and your IP address, as with any website request. Their handling is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Turn off Show cover art in the sidebar at any time to stop these requests.

The sample library ships with its cover links, so it never searches. Its images still load from Apple's image servers.

Stored in your browser

  • Cover image links that were already found, so they don't have to be searched again. They're saved under each song's title and artist and each album's name, so this list does reveal some of the songs in your library to anyone using this browser.
  • Whether you chose to show cover art.

These stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. Use Clear saved covers & settings in the sidebar to delete them, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
